Kanji Study — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Overview

Kanji Study is designed with your privacy in mind. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data whatsoever.

Data Storage

All app data — including your learning progress, preferences, and practice history — is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data is not accessible to us or any third party.

No Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information, usage analytics, crash reports, or device identifiers. No data is sent to any server at any time.

No Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Handwriting recognition is performed entirely on your device using Apple's built-in Vision framework.

No Internet Required

Kanji Study is fully functional without an internet connection. The app does not make any network requests.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from any user, including children. It is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
