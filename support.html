Frequently Asked Questions

How does daily practice work?

Each day, the app assigns 3 kanji to practice. You write each one during 3 sessions (morning, afternoon, evening). If you write a kanji correctly in all 3 sessions, it's marked as learned. Any kanji you miss will come back the next day.

How does handwriting recognition work?

The app uses Apple's built-in Vision framework to recognize your handwriting entirely on your device. No data is sent to any server. For best results, write each stroke clearly and try to match the correct stroke count.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The app works completely offline. All kanji data, stroke order guides, and handwriting recognition run on your device.

How do I change the JLPT level?

On the Study tab, use the level selector to choose your JLPT level (N5 through N1). This also sets which level the daily practice draws kanji from.

What is the lock screen widget?

You can add a Kanji Study widget to your lock screen or home screen. It displays a rotating kanji from your selected JLPT level. Long-press your lock screen and tap "Customize" to add it.
